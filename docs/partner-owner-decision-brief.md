## Purpose

The first-five qualification work produced two legitimate next routes: AWS Activate for infrastructure credits and Maccabi World Union for a bounded non-cash distribution-and-archive pilot. The remaining three researched targets stay on hold because no appropriate public commercial-intake route is verified.

This brief reduces the next owner review to the decisions that actually change execution. The recommended defaults preserve cash, editorial independence and rights discipline while avoiding premature external activity.

## Decision 1 — founding-partner position

**Recommended default:** retain the USD 30,000 twelve-month founding-partner position as the internal commercial anchor, but do not use it in either of the two first routes.

- AWS Activate is a credits application, not a sponsorship sale.
- The Maccabi World Union proposal is an eight-week non-cash pilot, not a founding-partner package.
- Keep the USD 30,000 position unchanged until measured audience and delivery evidence justify a pricing revision.
- Any discount, exclusivity or category lock-out remains a separate owner decision.

**Owner decision:** retain USD 30,000 / revise before outreach / pause commercial pricing.

## Decision 3 — Maccabi World Union pilot scope

**Recommended default:** approve the local pilot outline in principle with the following fixed boundaries.

- Eight consecutive weeks after a written start date.
- One or two English-speaking territorial organisations, selected only after interest is confirmed.
- Eight weekly briefings, two original archive features, four quizzes, one pilot landing page and one final aggregated report.
- No cash ask in the opening proposal.
- No exclusivity, editorial approval, automatic renewal or subscriber-level data sharing.
- Every logo, photograph, archive record, quotation and video requires item-specific rights clearance.
- Maccabi World Union may correct descriptions of its own programmes but cannot influence independent ILSP coverage.

**Owner decision:** approve these boundaries / revise deliverables or territory count / hold the route.

## Decision 4 — sender and material set

No external step can begin until the owner approves all four items together:

1. The named recipient or official contact route.
2. The sender identity and authenticated ILSP mailbox.
3. The exact controlled ask and attachment set.
4. The public or protected website URL the recipient will see.

**Recommended material set for later owner-approved use:**

- premium one-page founding-partner brief as background, clearly separated from the non-cash pilot;
- the eight-week pilot outline;
- a small current product screenshot set;
- privacy, corrections and commercial-independence links on the approved preview;
- no general sponsor deck unless the recipient asks for commercial options.
