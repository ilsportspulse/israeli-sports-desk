# Israel Sports Pulse — scalable partner outreach plan

Status: internal draft. Nothing in this document authorises sending, account creation, pricing commitments or external publication.

## Objective

Build a broad twelve-month partner pipeline without turning ILSP into a mass-mail campaign. The same general deck supports every route, but the opening message, benefit, ask and attachment choice must be specific to the organisation.

The operating target is a planning funnel, not a revenue forecast:

| Stage | Twelve-month planning range | Control |
|---|---:|---|
| Target universe researched | 500 organisations | Relevant category and current evidence |
| Personalised first approaches | 300 | Verified route, specific fit and owner-approved batch |
| Replies or useful referrals | 30–60 | Record outcome; do not inflate soft replies |
| Meetings or application reviews | 15–30 | Named objective and decision process |
| Proposals or bounded pilots | 6–12 | Exact price, term, rights and measurement |
| Funded or in-kind partners | 3–6 | Signed confirmation only |

These ranges imply 25 personalised openings per month. They must be replaced by actual conversion data once outreach begins.

## Five partner routes

1. **Founding cash and product sponsors** — banks, payments, telecom, travel, sportswear, consumer technology and professional services.
2. **Infrastructure and data** — cloud, cybersecurity, database, email, monitoring, sports data and image-rights providers.
3. **Mission and grant funders** — Jewish-community, diaspora, accessibility, journalism, youth, women’s sport and Israel-connection programmes.
4. **Distribution partners** — federations, communities, clubs, alumni networks, universities and sports organisations.
5. **Launch services in kind** — legal, accounting, privacy, design, photography, production and research support.

## Qualification before contact

Every row must have:

- organisation, category and current programme;
- official evidence URL and last-checked date;
- correct functional route or verified current role;
- one ILSP product and one partner outcome;
- exact cash, in-kind, grant, distribution or pilot ask;
- conflict, exclusivity, rights and brand-safety notes;
- owner approval for the recipient and wording;
- consent/unsubscribe status and the next action.

No guessed employee emails, scraped personal data or customer-service routes are used.

## Attachment rule

- A first cold message should normally link or attach only the approved one-page partner brief.
- The full private deck is shared after a reply, warm introduction or relevant application stage.
- A data/provider route receives the requirements matrix, not a generic sponsorship deck.
- A community route receives the bounded pilot outline.
- Never attach localhost screenshots, an outdated deck or a file with unapproved personal details.

After Day 10, stop unless the prospect replies or a genuinely material new development creates a fresh reason to contact them.

## Announcement after a signed partnership

No announcement is drafted as final until the agreement defines the name, marks, quotation approval, date, channels and disclosure wording. A safe announcement structure is:

1. verified partnership and term;
2. exact product or cost being supported;
3. useful reader outcome;
4. explicit editorial-independence sentence;
5. approved quotations from both sides;
6. start date and relevant product link.

Never announce a conversation, application, unsigned commitment or expected funding as a partnership.

## Metrics to report

- delivered placements and uptime;
- page, section, newsletter or Match Centre usage;
- viewability and click-through where lawful;
- new and returning users;
- geographic mix only at a privacy-safe aggregate level;
- quiz, archive, event or community participation;
- under-covered sport, women and youth output where relevant;
- corrections, complaints and brand-safety incidents;
- next-quarter tests and decisions.

Until analytics are live, the proposal promises deliverables and a measurement baseline—not a guaranteed audience number.
