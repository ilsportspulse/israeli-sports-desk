## Registration controls

- Register domains in an ILSP-controlled account, never in an agency, freelancer or sponsor account.
- Enable registry lock where available, DNSSEC, automatic renewal and account-level two-factor authentication.
- Store backup codes outside the primary computer and document the legal registrant.
- Use a shared operational recovery address controlled by the owner.
- Redirect secondary domains permanently to the primary domain after launch.
- Do not point public DNS at the local preview.
