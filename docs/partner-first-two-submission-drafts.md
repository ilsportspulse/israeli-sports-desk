### Form-ready company description

Israel Sports Pulse is an independent, mobile-first English sports publication covering Israeli leagues, national teams, athletes competing abroad and major international events. The working product combines a professionally gated newsroom, match and score interfaces, original analysis, a daily source-backed quiz and a permanent historical archive. The product is currently developed locally and will move to production only after its domain, legal, privacy, security and owner-controlled account gates are complete.

### Form-ready problem statement

Israeli sport produces a high volume of reporting, results and institutional information, but international supporters often lack one reliable English destination covering the full Israeli sports system. Israel Sports Pulse is designed to translate that need into original English journalism, structured score and article data, interactive match recaps and a durable archive without republishing source copy or sacrificing editorial independence.

### Form-ready AWS use case

AWS infrastructure would support the Next.js application and permitted newsroom workers, scheduled source discovery, structured article and score data, licensed media storage, delivery, monitoring, secrets and validated backups. The provisional architecture evaluates App Runner or ECS on Fargate, EventBridge with Lambda or container tasks, PostgreSQL through RDS or Aurora Serverless v2, S3, CloudFront, Route 53, Certificate Manager, CloudWatch, Budgets and Secrets Manager. Final services will be selected only after a small cost, portability and operations test.

### Form-ready reason for applying

Activate credits would reduce the cost of establishing a secure, monitored production foundation while ILSP validates audience demand and a sustainable operating model. The twelve-month planning forecast is USD 2,110 in potentially eligible AWS usage. Credits would not replace cost controls: the project will retain exportable article, score and media records, monthly budget review and an owner-approved spending ceiling so the architecture remains portable after any credit period ends.

### Subject

Eight-week English Israeli-sport pilot for Maccabi communities

### Contact-form or email draft

Hello [CURRENT DEVELOPMENT RECIPIENT],

I am building Israel Sports Pulse, an independent English sports publication covering Israeli leagues, national teams, athletes abroad and Israeli sporting history for readers in Israel and connected communities worldwide.

I would like to explore a bounded eight-week pilot with Maccabi World Union and one or two English-speaking territorial organisations. ILSP would produce eight mobile-first weekly briefings, two original archive features, four source-backed quiz activations, a clearly labelled pilot page and an aggregated end-of-pilot report. We would ask Maccabi World Union to nominate one programme contact, introduce two suitable territorial or community leads, consider distributing the weekly link through agreed existing channels and identify authoritative archive records where useful.

The opening proposal is non-cash. It provides no exclusivity, editorial approval or access to subscriber-level data. ILSP would retain complete control over stories, rankings, conclusions and corrections. Every logo, photograph, archive item, quotation or video would require separate written permission before use.

The purpose is to test whether a concise English Israeli-sport product creates repeat, useful engagement in a clearly defined audience. Success would be measured through delivery, actual distribution, privacy-safe aggregated engagement, rights compliance and the quality of the final stop, repeat or redesign decision—not through an audience promise made in advance.

Would the development team be open to a short introductory conversation after reviewing the one-page pilot outline? If so, I can share the current product preview and proposed eight-week scope.

Best,
