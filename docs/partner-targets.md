# Israel Sports Pulse — partner research board

Status: internal research. No organisation has been contacted and no relationship is implied.

The structured working list is maintained in `data/partner-prospects.json`. It deliberately records organisations and official public routes rather than guessed employee names or scraped personal details. A named recipient is added only after current role verification.

## Recommended order

### Wave 1 — reduce unavoidable costs

1. **AWS Activate** — cloud credits.
2. **Cloudflare for Startups** — delivery, security and platform credits.
3. **Sportmonks** — proof-of-coverage and startup data proposal.
4. **API-Football** — price and coverage benchmark.

These conversations must establish the real post-credit price, expiry, data-display rights, team-logo rights, refresh limits, Israeli league coverage, cancellation terms and export path. Free access is not valuable if it creates a migration trap.

### Wave 2 — founding cash partners

1. **Bank Hapoalim** — founding partner or Match Centre.
2. **Bank Leumi** — founding partner, Daily Five or multilingual access.
3. **yes** — video or major-event product, after the correct partnership route is verified.

The initial cash proposal is not a request to “help a website”. It is a twelve-month audience-and-utility partnership with named inventory, reporting, editorial separation and a credible product already running locally.

### Wave 3 — distribution and mission partners

1. **Maccabi World Union** — global sport network, archive and introductions.
2. **Jewish Agency Partnership2Gether** — community pilots and diaspora introductions.
3. **Jewish Federations of North America** — selected federation pilots.

These organisations should first receive a small, concrete pilot: a local community sports page, a weekly Israel sports briefing, or an archive-and-quiz activation. The partnership should not imply endorsement of ILSP reporting.

## Qualification gate

A prospect becomes outreach-ready only when all eight fields are complete:

1. Current organisation and programme verified on an official site.
2. Named decision-maker or correct functional inbox verified.
3. One specific ILSP product chosen.
4. Cash or in-kind ask stated exactly.
5. Prospect benefit stated in one sentence.
6. Conflict and exclusivity risks recorded.
7. Owner approves recipient and message.
8. Authenticated ILSP mailbox is ready with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.

## Pipeline stages

`research` → `qualified` → `owner-approved` → `contacted` → `replied` → `meeting` → `proposal` → `legal` → `won/lost`

No automated system may advance a prospect beyond `replied` or agree to money, exclusivity, data rights, editorial access, privacy terms or legal terms.
