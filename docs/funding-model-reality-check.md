# Israel Sports Pulse — funding-model reality check

Reviewed: 17 July 2026  
Status: internal planning review; not an accounting, tax, employment or investment opinion.

## Conclusion

The professional scenario is mathematically correct and usable as a **conservative founder-led twelve-month launch model**. It is not a fully staffed corporate newsroom budget and should never be described that way.

The recommended cash target is USD 213,790:

| Component | Professional scenario |
|---|---:|
| Monthly operating run rate | USD 14,465 |
| Twelve-month operating cost | USD 173,580 |
| One-time launch work | USD 17,304 |
| Contingency reserve | USD 22,906 |
| **Twelve-month cash target** | **USD 213,790** |
| Separate indicative in-kind target | USD 30,000 |

The workbook checks reconcile every scenario, the professional monthly total and the contingency calculation. The formula-error scan returned no errors.

## What is currently realistic

- Public platform starting prices are within the model: [Vercel Pro](https://vercel.com/pricing) at USD 20/month, [Supabase Pro](https://supabase.com/pricing) from USD 25/month, [Resend Pro](https://resend.com/pricing?volume=50000) at USD 20/month for 50,000 transactional emails, [Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/whats-included/) at USD 99/year and [Google Play registration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14659200?hl=en) at a one-time USD 25.
- Football-data entry prices remain far below the professional budget allowance: [API-Football](https://www.api-football.com/pricing) lists USD 19/29/39 monthly plans, while [Sportmonks](https://www.sportmonks.com/football-api/plans-pricing/) starts at EUR 29 for five leagues and EUR 99 for thirty leagues.
- The USD 250 professional football-data allowance therefore leaves room for higher league count, add-ons or a second source, but publication and logo rights still require review.
- The 12 per cent contingency is reasonable for an early-stage operation with unresolved data, media, legal and infrastructure quotes.
- The model excludes unconfirmed advertising, grants and in-kind support from available cash, which prevents unsupported revenue from lowering the funding requirement.

## What remains an estimate or risk

- Founder, editorial, contributor, audience, commercial and development lines assume a blended founder/freelance/part-time structure. Employer taxes, employee benefits, pension, equipment and jurisdiction-specific obligations are not included.
- The USD 14,465 run rate would be too low for six full-time senior hires. It is credible only if roles are fractional, contracted, founder-covered or partly in kind during year one.
- Israeli multi-sport data, image rights, domestic action photography, insurance, legal, accounting and production support remain quote-dependent. Any one of these could materially exceed the professional assumption.
- The sponsor prices are inventory hypotheses, not market-validated revenue. USD 30,000 for a founding position can be presented as an anchor, but no audience reach or conversion should be guaranteed before production analytics exist.
- The current model is a cost-and-funding model, not a cash-flow forecast. It does not yet model payment timing, VAT/tax, bad debt, payroll timing, receivables or monthly fundraising conversion.

## Recommended external wording

Use:

> USD 213,790 is the recommended twelve-month founder-led professional launch target. The model reconciles, public platform fees have been rechecked, and unresolved employment, media and data costs remain subject to local quotations. It is a planning case, not a commitment or revenue forecast.

Do not use:

- “fully funded newsroom” unless staffing and employer costs are quoted;
- “guaranteed reach”, “guaranteed return” or invented audience figures;
- “three founding partners secured” before signed agreements;
- in-kind value as cash;
- an application, conversation or verbal interest as a partnership.

## Commercial stress test

The illustrative USD 212,000 cash stack in the workbook is close to the USD 213,790 target, but it assumes successful outcomes across founding partners, product partners, grants, members/donors and an event route. It is a design example, not a forecast.

A safer operating strategy is:

1. pursue hundreds of personalised prospects across cash, grant, in-kind, distribution and service categories;
2. secure one anchor cash partner and one infrastructure/data partner before production purchases;
3. use bounded pilots when audience evidence is not yet mature;
4. replace planning conversion ranges with real CRM data after the first fifty approved approaches;
5. update the model with signed timing, taxes and quotes before treating the target as a cash plan.
