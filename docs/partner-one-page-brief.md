# Israel Sports Pulse - Founding Partner Brief

## The opportunity

Israel Sports Pulse (ILSP) is building a premium English-language destination for Israeli sport: professionally gated reporting, live match utility, original columns, a daily Israeli-sport quiz and a growing historical archive. The product already operates as a polished local preview and is being prepared for a controlled public launch.

## Founding Partner proposal

**USD 30,000 for twelve months.** Up to three non-competing founding positions are planned. Pricing and inventory remain draft until owner approval and contract.

The partner receives:

- clearly labelled launch recognition across agreed website and app surfaces;
- presence in the launch campaign and partner page;
- an agreed newsletter or product placement, separated from editorial copy;
- quarterly delivery and engagement reporting once measurement is live;
- first discussion rights on a relevant product package at renewal, without editorial or category-control rights.

## What the funding unlocks

- Licensed Israeli and international sports data, scores, tables and statistics.
- Rights-cleared action photography and broader coverage beyond top-flight football and basketball.
- Reliable hosting, security, monitoring, backups, email and audience infrastructure.
- Limited professional editorial, engineering and commercial capacity.
- The staged foundation for iOS, Android and any future language edition selected for a distinct audience after owner approval; Hebrew translation is not part of the current roadmap.

## Funding context

The current professional model estimates a **USD 213,790 twelve-month cash requirement**, including launch costs and contingency, plus a **USD 30,000 in-kind target**. A founding partnership funds a defined part of that runway; it is not presented as sufficient to finance the entire publication.

## Editorial firewall

Funding never includes story approval, source selection, favourable coverage, ranking influence, corrections control or subscriber-data access. Sponsored inventory is clearly labelled. Youth products carry no betting promotion. Conflicts and in-kind support are recorded.

## Audience proposition

ILSP is designed for English-reading sports audiences in Israel and for Jewish and Israel-connected communities worldwide. No public reach or audience figures are claimed before production analytics exist.

**Suggested next step:** a 30-minute launch, inventory and strategic-fit review after recipient, pricing and sender identity receive owner approval.
