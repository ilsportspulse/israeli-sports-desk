# Israel Sports Pulse — controlled outreach kit

Status: draft only. Nothing in this document authorises sending.

The scalable twelve-month funnel, attachment rules, full personalisable messages and announcement structure are maintained in `docs/partner-outreach-scale-plan.md`. The current operating assumption is 500 researched organisations, 300 personalised first approaches and 3–6 funded or in-kind outcomes; these are workload ranges, not a revenue forecast.

## Before the first message

- Register the approved domain in an owner-controlled account.
- Configure `partners@ilsportspulse.com` with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
- Add a real legal sender name, business address where required, privacy notice and simple signature.
- Publish a sponsor-safe preview or protected presentation URL; never send a localhost link.
- Verify the recipient's current role using the organisation's site and one second current source.
- Personalise the product, audience fit and ask; never send a generic mass campaign.
- Attach or link only the latest approved deck and funding summary.

## Follow-up sequence

- **Day 0:** personalised opening message.
- **Day 4:** one short follow-up with a relevant product screenshot or verified milestone.
- **Day 10:** final follow-up with one concrete meeting window and graceful close.
- **After Day 10:** close the sequence unless the prospect engages or a genuinely new development justifies contact.

## Reply triage

### May be drafted automatically

- Acknowledge receipt.
- Offer owner-approved meeting windows.
- Share the approved deck, product preview or requirements matrix.
- Answer factual questions already documented in approved materials.

### Must wait for the owner

- Price, discount or commission.
- Exclusivity, category lock-out or first-refusal rights.
- Contract, invoice, payment or legal entity details.
- Data-processing, privacy or security commitments.
- Editorial access, content approval or placement guarantees.
- Public announcement, logo usage or quotation approval.

## Meeting agenda

1. Prospect goal and audience.
2. ILSP product demonstration.
3. Exact product or cost being funded.
4. Inventory and measurement.
5. Editorial and data-rights boundaries.
6. Budget, term and decision process.
7. Named next action and date.

## CRM minimum fields

Organisation, category, named contact, role-verification URL, work email source, fit, exact ask, product, estimated value, conflict notes, owner approval, last contact, next action, stage, consent/unsubscribe status and decision record.
