# Israel Sports Pulse — zero-cash funding plan

ILSP is the property seeking money and in-kind support. It will not pay to sponsor other organisations. No external message, purchase, commercial commitment or account registration is made without owner approval.

## Funding objective

Secure enough cash and donated services to cover:

- production hosting, storage, security, monitoring and backups;
- licensed sports-data feeds and permitted logo/media display;
- photography or editorial-image access;
- domain, email, app-store and necessary legal costs;
- audience development and newsletter/push infrastructure;
- limited editorial, engineering and commercial compensation;
- a contingency reserve for feed, hosting or legal incidents.

The first commercial target is a funded twelve-month launch runway, not a vanity announcement. The exact target will be set after the cost workbook and provider quotations are complete.

## Revenue and support routes

### 1. Founding partners

Cash support in exchange for prominent, clearly labelled brand recognition and first access to defined commercial inventory. Founding status never includes story approval, source selection or influence over rankings.

### 2. Product sponsorships

- Match Centre
- Daily Five quiz
- From the Archive
- Morning Pulse newsletter
- Israeli Athletes Abroad
- Mobile score alerts
- Future iOS and Android applications

Each product has a clear term, placement list, audience promise, reporting method and editorial-separation clause.

### 3. In-kind infrastructure partners

Seek donated or heavily discounted hosting, sports data, image access, email, analytics, monitoring, legal review and creative production. Record the fair commercial value and expiry of every credit so ILSP does not inherit an unaffordable service after launch.

### 4. Grants and community funding

Research journalism, sports-development, accessibility, multilingual media, Jewish-community and Israel-diaspora programmes whose rules permit independent media work. Never reshape news coverage to satisfy a funder’s political or institutional agenda.

### 5. Audience revenue

Later-stage options include voluntary membership, a supporter tier, events and premium alerts. Core breaking news, corrections and essential public-interest reporting should not be restricted merely to manufacture subscriptions.

## Partner categories to research

- Israeli banks and payment companies
- telecom and broadband providers
- television, streaming and media platforms
- airlines, travel and tourism brands
- cloud, cybersecurity and software companies
- sportswear, fitness and health brands
- universities and sports-science institutions
- Jewish federations, foundations and community organisations
- organisations supporting Israeli athletes and Olympic sport
- professional-service firms able to contribute legal, accounting or commercial support

Research must identify the correct current decision-maker, a legitimate business contact route, strategic fit, conflicts and the exact value ILSP can offer. Generic mass emailing is prohibited.

## Editorial photography procurement

ILSP should use a layered image model rather than depend on one expensive wire service:

1. Keep Wikimedia Commons and Openverse as the free discovery layer, with every individual file licence verified at source before use.
2. Test a small IMAGO credit pack for priority international events. Its entry package was listed at $99 when checked on 15 July 2026; no purchase is authorised until image coverage and per-use terms are reviewed against ILSP's actual needs.
3. Seek a direct monthly editorial agreement with an Israeli sports photographer or agency covering domestic football, basketball and smaller sports. The agreement must cover website, app, newsletter and social use, archive retention, cropping, credit wording and any geographic or audience limits.
4. Use Shutterstock Editorial or Alamy only for exceptional gaps unless a negotiated package materially lowers the per-story price. Editorial licences are normally use-specific and must not be treated as general brand, advertising or merchandise rights.
5. Ask a founding or in-kind partner to underwrite the domestic photography pool as a ring-fenced launch cost without receiving editorial influence.

Every licensed download must retain the invoice or agreement, asset identifier, allowed uses, licence period, photographer credit and proof of any permitted adaptation in the internal media ledger.

## Outreach workflow

1. Verify the organisation and current decision-maker.
2. Record why the prospect fits one specific ILSP product.
3. Prepare a personalised message and concrete funding or in-kind ask.
4. Obtain owner approval for the recipient list and campaign.
5. Send through an authenticated ILSP mailbox.
6. Track opens only where lawful and proportionate; prioritise replies and meetings.
7. Follow up twice with useful new information, then close the sequence.
8. Escalate pricing, exclusivity, data access, legal terms and editorial requests to the owner.

Automated replies may acknowledge receipt and schedule a meeting. They may not agree to price, exclusivity, deliverables, editorial access, data processing or legal terms.

## Non-negotiable protections

- Sponsored placements are visibly labelled.
- Funders cannot approve, delay or suppress editorial work.
- No sponsor receives favourable news treatment.
- Youth products do not carry betting promotion.
- Corrections, safety and public-interest reporting cannot be blocked commercially.
- Conflicts are disclosed and documented.
- In-kind support is treated as a commercial relationship and recorded transparently.
