# Israel Sports Pulse — first-five partner qualification

Status date: 16 July 2026  
Visibility: internal research only. No organisation has been contacted and no relationship is implied.

## Decision summary

This round separates a good brand fit from a legitimate outreach route. AWS Activate and Maccabi World Union have current official paths that can be prepared for owner approval. The public sponsorship or donation routes at Bank Hapoalim and Bank Leumi are not appropriate for ILSP as an independent commercial media project. A follow-up route audit verified the relevant internal procurement and marketing functions at both banks, but found no public unsolicited commercial-intake path. yes remains strategically attractive; the official Bezeq supplier portal and B2B reseller programme were checked and ruled out because neither accepts sponsorship or media-partnership proposals.

## Evidence and reasoning

### AWS Activate

- Current official credits page: https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/
- AWS describes a direct Founders route for self-funded startups, starting with USD 1,000 and with selected participants potentially receiving up to USD 5,000.
- Published eligibility includes pre-Series B, founded within ten years, a paid-tier AWS account, and being new to Activate credits or requesting a higher tier.
- A functioning company website and consistent professional identity are operational prerequisites. ILSP should apply only when it can use the credits efficiently before expiry.

### Maccabi World Union

- Official staff page: https://www.maccabi.org/who-we-are/staff
- Official contact page: https://www.maccabi.org/contact
- Official partners page: https://www.maccabi.org/who-we-are/partners
- The staff page identifies Adi Ratzon as Director of Development. The organisation reports a global network of clubs and territorial organisations, making distribution and archive collaboration a much stronger opening than a generic funding request.
- The first proposal should be a bounded pilot with audience, term, rights, outputs and measurement agreed in advance.

### Bank Hapoalim

- Official 2025 ESG report: https://www.bankhapoalim.co.il/sites/default/files/media/PDFS/SocialBanking/ESG_poalim_2025.pdf
- Official organisation profile: https://www.bankhapoalim.co.il/he/about/bank-profile
- The report records approximately NIS 39 million of community investment in 2025, but also states that donations are for registered nonprofits/public-benefit companies and event sponsorships for nonprofits or professional bodies, subject to anti-bribery controls.
- The organisation profile places Marketing and Advertising HQ under the chief executive. The ESG report identifies Corporate Procurement as the bank-wide sourcing function and describes a structured process built around an existing supplier database and requests for proposals.
- Conclusion: the correct function pair is now verified, but a public intake is not. The public community route is not a shortcut to media funding, and press or investor-relations contacts are not substitutes.

### Bank Leumi

- Official ESG page: https://www.leumi.co.il/en/Lobby-Investor-Relations/ESG
- Official donations and sponsorship page: https://www.leumi.co.il/he/about-leumi/about_corporate-responsibility/Donations_and_sponsorship
- Current supplier code: https://www.leumi.co.il/leumi-hebrew/s3fs-public/complex_link_file/02-25/Leumi_Way.acc.pdf
- Official procurement classification: https://www.leumi.co.il/leumi-hebrew/s3fs-public/complex_link_file/11-24/Leumi_ESG_Report_2023_Acc.pdf
- Leumi reports NIS 48 million invested in community initiatives in 2025 and describes donations/sponsorship decisions through a bank committee for public bodies and nonprofits.
- The supplier code says the Procurement Division locates, screens, prices, negotiates and manages suppliers. Leumi's official procurement reporting includes marketing and advertising within general procurement, confirming the route is a commercial purchase rather than a donation.
- Conclusion: the project should be pitched as measurable commercial inventory through a marketing owner and Procurement, but no public unsolicited supplier intake is verified.

### yes

- Official Bezeq 2025 report: https://ir.bezeq.co.il/static-files/c7df9a10-1111-4e29-a396-f440a4146399
- Official Bezeq supplier portal: https://suppliers.bezeq.co.il/
- Official Bezeq Business partner programme: https://www.bezeq.co.il/business/bservices_gallery/b2b/
- The report confirms yes markets through media, digital channels, call centres and field sales, but those consumer routes do not establish a commercial-partnership contact.
- The supplier portal is an account path for suppliers, while the business-partner programme targets IT, computing, consulting and integration firms and pays commission for leads, completed sales and customer support. Both are documented false routes for ILSP's sponsorship proposal.
- A future approach should specify legal video rights, connected-TV distribution, campaign inventory, reporting and complete editorial separation, but it must wait for a current yes marketing or business-development intake.

## Owner gates

Before any submission or message, the owner must approve the recipient, ask, attached materials and sender identity. A real ILSP domain, authenticated mailbox, legal sender details and a sponsor-safe public or protected preview must exist. No draft may promise exclusivity, editorial access, audience numbers that have not been measured, or rights ILSP does not hold.
