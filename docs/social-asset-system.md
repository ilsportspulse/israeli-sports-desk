# Israel Sports Pulse — reusable social asset system

Status date: 16 July 2026  
Visibility: internal production guide. No account has been created and no post is authorised.

## Delivered templates

| Template | Canvas | Primary use |
|---|---:|---|
| `assets/social/templates/ilsp-story-card-4x5.svg` | 1080 × 1350 | Instagram feed, Facebook and LinkedIn story cards |
| `assets/social/templates/ilsp-score-card-square.svg` | 1080 × 1080 | Confirmed results, fixtures and score updates |
| `assets/social/templates/ilsp-vertical-story.svg` | 1080 × 1920 | Instagram Stories, TikTok/Reels covers and vertical explainers |

The files are master SVGs. Replace bracketed fields and the media placeholder in a derivative file; never overwrite the master. Keep the brand navy, Israeli blue, white and cool-grey palette. Lime, fluorescent yellow and unrelated club colours are not masterbrand accents.

## Image treatment

- Use one exact, rights-cleared photograph or verified editorial visual per derivative.
- Preserve the complete frame. Fit the image inside the marked photo area and use the navy fade for legibility; do not crop out the subject to fill every edge.
- Keep the photographer, source, licence, source URL, capture date, depicted event and modifications in the media ledger.
- A file photograph must say when and where it was taken. It must not be presented as the current match.
- A social or publisher photograph is not reusable merely because it can be found online.

## Copy limits

- Category or format label: maximum 24 characters.
- Feed headline: preferably 55–82 characters and no more than three display lines.
- Vertical headline: no more than three display lines.
- Context line: one verified consequence, score detail or next event; never repeat the headline.
- Score cards use confirmed match status, Jerusalem time and verified scorers. Remove unused fields rather than publishing placeholders.
- Publisher names, verification mechanics and automation language never appear in reader-facing graphics.

## Export rules

- Feed story: PNG or high-quality JPEG at 1080 × 1350.
- Square result: PNG at 1080 × 1080.
- Vertical: PNG/JPEG cover at 1080 × 1920; H.264 video derivatives only from owned or licensed footage.
- Use sRGB, keep text inside the template safe area and visually inspect every export at phone size.
- Archive the editable derivative, exported file, caption copy and rights record under the same content ID.

## Publication gate

Before any post: verify the story is still current, the link is public, the asset matches the event, the score and time are correct, the caption is source-backed, the alt text is useful and the owner-approved account is being used. Accounts, posts and scheduling remain external approval actions.
