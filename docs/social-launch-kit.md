## Editorial formats

- **Pulse Alert:** one verified development, exact time, link and what changes next.
- **Three Things:** a compact post-match or transfer explainer.
- **Israeli Abroad:** the athlete, competition, result and next event.
- **Match Card:** teams, crests, confirmed status, score, scorers and link.
- **On This Day:** one researched historical event with a primary source.
- **Daily Five:** one quiz teaser; answers remain in the interactive product.
- **Morning Pulse:** five essential developments, not five publisher headlines.
- **Why It Matters:** a 30–60 second original video or four-panel carousel.

## Publishing rules

- Never publish a source image unless its reuse rights are documented.
- Use a different, genuinely matching image for every story card.
- Link to ILSP reporting; use natural in-article attribution rather than publisher promotion.
- Confirm teams, identities, score, scorers and match status before posting.
- State uncertainty plainly; delete-and-hide is not a correction policy.
- Corrections are timestamped and visible.
- No betting prompts in youth products or the Daily Five.
- No sponsor can approve or suppress a post.
- Do not auto-translate a public channel without native review.

## Account controls

Owner-controlled recovery email and phone, unique password, two-factor authentication, offline backup codes, two administrators where the platform permits, a monthly access review and a written recovery owner. Never register an account in a freelancer, agency or sponsor identity.
