# Israel Sports Pulse — autonomous operations roadmap

## Continuous schedule

- Every 30 minutes: source discovery, permission checks, deduplication and review-queue update, followed by one bounded launch-roadmap task whenever the newsroom gate is complete.
- At least hourly: promoted-story, freshness, duplicate and media audit.
- At the licensed provider interval: scores, incidents and tables refresh.
- Daily: archive feature, five-question quiz, image audit and newsletter preparation.
- Daily: update the internal project board with completed evidence, current blockers and the next executable task.
- Weekly: funding pipeline, operating-cost, source health and technical-quality review.
- Monthly: audience, revenue, costs, corrections, uptime and product-performance report.

The newsroom and launch roadmap are parallel workstreams. A quiet news cycle must produce tangible local progress on the product, funding case, social launch, partner research, scores architecture, applications or multilingual foundation. Planning alone does not count when a safe implementation artifact can be completed.

## Authority boundaries

Local research, code, design, testing, draft content, draft presentations and internal planning can proceed autonomously. Production deployment, purchases, domain registration, external account creation, outbound messages, contracts, payments and publication outside the local preview require owner approval.

## Phase order

1. Brand, rollback safety and operating automation.
2. Newsroom quality and source reliability.
3. Licensed scores and statistics architecture.
4. Mobile-first product and audience features.
5. Funding model, cost workbook, media kit and presentation.
6. Approved account registration and controlled outreach.
7. Production, applications and public launch.

The living status and evidence board is maintained in `docs/project-board.md`, with a machine-readable mirror in `data/project-progress.json`.

## Quality gate

No feature is considered complete until it has proportionate automated checks, desktop and mobile inspection, an honest unavailable state, documented ownership and a rollback path.
